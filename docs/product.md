# Product contract

master-spec.txt remains the full target. The primary path is channel URL -> RSS discovery -> persistent queue -> platform transcript -> user-selected local AI -> validated drafts or MP4 exports. Auto Draft and Auto Export are distinct; pause preserves mode across restart, and missing model/captions requires input. ASR never starts automatically.

The native sidebar exposes Home, Sources, Clips and Settings. Local video editing remains available as a secondary action. Exported automatic clips are center-cropped to 1080×1920 H.264/AAC MP4 without a watermark. AI drafts can open their original source with the proposed time range for manual editing.

No social publishing, account linking, SaaS, web shell, mandatory telemetry or AppImage. Remote BYOK providers, Kick, richer editing, ASR, full accessibility/usability studies and signed installers remain part of the full specification and are not complete.
