# Product contract

master-spec.txt remains the full target. The primary path is channel URL -> RSS discovery -> persistent queue -> platform transcript -> user-selected local AI -> validated drafts or MP4 exports. Auto Draft and Auto Export are distinct; pause preserves mode across restart, and missing model/captions requires input. ASR never starts automatically.

The native sidebar exposes Home, Clips, Studio, Automations, Connections, Settings and About, with a search box that opens the Ctrl+K palette. The Studio tab holds the player, timeline and caption editor. Exported automatic clips are center-cropped to 1080×1920 H.264/AAC MP4 without a watermark. AI drafts can open their original source with the proposed time range for manual editing.

No social publishing, account linking, SaaS, web shell, mandatory telemetry or AppImage. Remote BYOK providers, a stable Kick integration, richer editing, ASR, full accessibility/usability studies and signed installers remain part of the full specification and are not complete.
